Little Things About You

"It is the quiet, unnoticed details that make you completely unforgettable."

Your Little Wishes

You remember the smallest wishes—a sunflower, a dolonchapa flower, a boat ride. Those gentle dreams reveal the softness of your heart.

🎨

Your Artistic Spirit

The mehendi drawn on your hands, your eye for colors and subtle beauty. You see poetry where others only see the mundane.

🌸

Your Love for Flowers

Dolonchapa, kadam, orchids, beli. You have an entire garden blossoming in your dreams, and I cherish each petal.

🌧

You and the Rain

Visiting TSC in the rain, longing for Shapla Bil during monsoons. Rain belongs to your happiest stories.
